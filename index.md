# Privacy Policy — Servers for minecraft

Effective date: 22 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to **Servers for minecraft** (package `com.fawora.servers`).

## Data processed by the app

The app does not create user accounts and does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, payment details, photos, messages, or other personal information.

The app retrieves a public catalogue of server content through HTTPS requests to `https://luckymods.ru/ru/wp-json/wp/v2/`. Those requests contain no account, advertising, analytics, or device-identifier fields added by the app. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, Firebase, AppMetrica, Google Play Billing, or the Android advertising ID.

The app does not collect, retain, sell, or share personal data. Standard network infrastructure used to deliver web content may process technical connection information, such as an IP address, as necessary to respond to a request; this app does not receive or store that information.

## Downloads and device storage

If a user chooses a catalogue file, the app downloads it to the user’s device. The app uses storage access only to save and open the file selected by the user. It does not upload files from the device.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
